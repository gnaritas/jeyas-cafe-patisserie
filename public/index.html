What kind of place is this?

  • Everything baked in-house. The viennoiserie comes out of JEYAS’s own ovens, not a delivery tray — the croissants are the whole point.
  • Parisian résumé, Sacramento roots. The pastry program is led by Sacramento-born chef Antonio Coriano, who trained in France and came back via a Michelin-starred kitchen.
  • Dawn to midnight. The plan is doors at 6:30 a.m. and crêpes until midnight — breakfast for the early risers, dessert for the later crowd.
  • An inside–outside café. About 1,500 square feet inside plus a patio of roughly 750–1,000 square feet at the Ice Blocks — outdoor seating is a big part of the experience.

“We are looking forward to opening a new place to welcome everyone to come and enjoy specialty croissants and specialty drinks.”

Yusuf Topal, co-owner — via What Now Sacramento

The chef

Antonio Coriano runs the pastry program: Sacramento-born, trained in France, ex–pastry chef de partie at Michelin-starred Protégé in Palo Alto, formerly executive pastry chef at Old Soul Co. and lead macaron baker at Ginger Elizabeth Chocolates.

Three daughters, one name

JEYAS blends the names of co-owner Yusuf’s three daughters — Jeyda, Yasemin and Ashley. The café is a family project, brothers Yusuf and Levent Topal, built for Sacramento’s everyday life.

The corner

The former Pressed Juicery storefront at the Ice Blocks, plus the leasing office next door — a Midtown development block that was built for exactly this kind of sidewalk café.
