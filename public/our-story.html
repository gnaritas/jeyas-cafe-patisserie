A FAMILY PROJECT, NAMED FOR THE FAMILY

Jeyda. Yasemin.
Ashley. JEYAS.

The name blends three daughters’ names into one storefront. The café comes from brothers Yusuf and Levent Topal — Sacramento restaurant people — and a pastry chef who grew up two neighborhoods from the corner they’re signing.

The family behind the counter

Yusuf Topal has run Sacramento restaurants for more than 30 years — he owns Jayna Gyro on Folsom Boulevard, and has operated Tarla Mediterranean Grill in Napa and Cellar at The Jacquelyn social club in Midtown. His brother Levent, years in hospitality himself, will run daily operations at JEYAS.

The café is the family’s newest project and its most personal one: the name is stitched together from Yusuf’s three daughters — Jeyda, Yasemin and Ashley.

The chef

Sacramento-born Antonio Coriano runs the pastry program. He trained in France, spent years as pastry chef de partie at Michelin-starred Protégé in Palo Alto, and before that ran pastry at Old Soul Co. and the macaron program at Ginger Elizabeth Chocolates. He came back to a city that already knows his desserts — and a corner that didn’t have a place like this yet.

The corner

The space is the former Pressed Juicery storefront at 1710 R Street — about 1,500 square feet inside — plus the leasing office next door, opening up a patio of roughly 750 to 1,000 square feet on the Ice Blocks’ promenade. Outdoor seating, the developer’s own word, is a big part of what the block is.
