THE LATE HALF OF THE DAY

Crêpes, from
morning to midnight

Most cafés are a breakfast business with a lunch problem. JEYAS is planned the other way around: doors at 6:30 a.m., and sweet and savory French-style crêpes — with dessert — on the menu until midnight, every day.

The Crêpe Board

Two boards, one batter program — the savory side for lunch, the sweet side that doubles as dinner and dessert.

  • Sweet CrêpesThe French dessert that happens to show up at breakfast too. The late-evening sweet tooth has an address now.
  • Savory CrêpesThe lunch plate — buckwheat-and-fillings territory, done as a café crêperie should.

Specific crêpe preparations haven’t been published yet — they’ll be announced with the final menu.

“Whether you’re an early riser looking for your first cup of coffee and a fresh croissant, meeting friends for breakfast or lunch, or satisfying a late-night dessert craving, we want JEYAS to be the place you think of.”

Yusuf Topal, co-owner — via The Abridged (PBS KVIE)

Why midnight?

The Ice Blocks block is Midtown’s densest stretch of evening foot traffic — restaurants, bars, and the apartments between them. The family’s plan: coffee and pastry in the morning hours, crêpes and desserts late into the night, with the patio open much of that window.

Hours are the announced plan, not yet a posted schedule — 6:30 a.m. to midnight daily, per the owners’ coverage. The confirmed schedule goes up here at opening.

A dessert list worth the drive at 11 p.m.?

That’s what the owners say they’re building. Opening late spring 2027 at 1710 R Street.
