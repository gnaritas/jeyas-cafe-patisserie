Where

1710 R Street, Suite 170

At the Ice Blocks in Midtown — the brick corridor between downtown and the Midtown grid, on R Street. The storefront that housed Pressed Juicery, plus the space next door, opening onto the block’s promenade.

When

Opening late spring 2027 — the date isn’t posted yet. The announced plan: open at 6:30 a.m., close at midnight, every day — coffee and pastry in the morning, crêpes and dessert late.

Good to know

  • Roughly 1,500 sq ft inside, plus a 750–1,000 sq ft patio — outdoor seating is central to the plan.
  • Everything baked in-house: specialty croissants, pastries, desserts.
  • Planned daily lineup: croissants & pastry · crêpes · specialty coffee · smoothies · açaí · sandwiches.
  • The family behind Jayna Gyro — same owners, a different kind of counter.

Hours, prices and the opening date are the announced plan and aren’t final — they’ll be posted here the day the shop confirms them.
