THE PLANNED LINEUP AT OPENING

La Carte

Everything below comes from the opening announcement and press coverage — the menu is still being finalized, and prices arrive at opening. Here’s what the family has said it plans to serve, and when.

Viennoiseries & Pâtisseries

The head of the café — everything out of JEYAS’s own ovens, from early morning.

  • Specialty CroissantsLaminated in-house, baked before dawn. “Specialty croissants” is the owners’ own phrase — this is the item the whole café is built around.
  • Parisian-Style PastriesThe European-style pastry program, led by chef Antonio Coriano — a decade and a half of pastry kitchens, from Protégé to Ginger Elizabeth.
  • DessertsBecause the plan is to keep serving long after most cafés close.

Crêpes

Sweet and savory, French-style — morning through midnight.

  • Sweet CrêpesThe dessert-to-breakfast spectrum, done properly.
  • Savory CrêpesThe lunch plate with a zinc-and-steam pedigree.

Café & Bowls
